## Abstract

This repository contains the Quarto RevealJS slide deck for **3-D Reconstruction and Printing Tools: Fundamentals**, prepared for the summer school AMARCH26 held at the University of Trento, June 2026. The material introduces the role of 3-D technologies in archaeological documentation and replication, then surveys reconstruction methods including photogrammetry, structured light, LiDAR, and time-of-flight scanning. It also covers precision and resolution concepts, solid model preparation, slicing, support generation, and major 3-D printing processes such as FDM, SLA, powder-bed systems, and metal additive manufacturing.
